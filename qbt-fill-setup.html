2 · Every day after that

  1. Work Reporter → Copy for timesheet fill.
  2. Open QuickBooks Time in this same browser (stay logged in as you).
  3. Click the QBT fill assist bookmark.
  4. Tap each value into the matching field. You submit.

Safari / phone
