2 · Every day after that

  1. Work Reporter → Copy for timesheet fill.
  2. Open QuickBooks Time in this same browser (stay logged in as you).
  3. Click the QBT fill assist bookmark — it auto-fills matching fields.
  4. Glance the form. You hit Save/Submit.

Safari / phone

Bookmark any page → Edit → paste the helper link as the address. On iPhone this is awkward; desktop Chrome/Edge is the reliable path. You can still paste the readable clipboard text into QBT Notes without the helper.
